# Outreach templates

Rules that decide whether these land or go to spam:

- **Max 12 sends per day from a personal Gmail.** Above roughly 20 cold sends/day a
  new sending pattern starts tripping filters. 12 is safe and is more than enough
  when each one is tailored.
- **No tracking pixels, no link shorteners, no attachments on the first mail to a
  person** (attachments to a jobs@ address are fine and expected).
- **Plain text.** No HTML signature blocks, no images.
- **One link maximum** in a cold email, and it is the LinkedIn profile:
  `linkedin.com/in/saifaslam246`. A recruiter checks LinkedIn first, and it
  carries the recommendations and history a GitHub profile does not. GitHub and the
  portfolio are already on the CV itself, so the email does not need to repeat them.
- **Subject lines under 60 characters**, no "Re:" fakery, no ALL CAPS, no emoji.
- **Send 08:00-10:00 local time of the recipient**, Tuesday to Thursday best.
- Every mail names something specific about *their* product. A template that could
  have been sent to anyone reads as spam to both the filter and the human.

## 3. Warm follow-up - no reply after 5 working days

**Subject:** reply in the same thread, do not change the subject

```
Hi {NAME},

Following up on this in case it got buried. Still interested in {ROLE}, and
still have capacity from {DATE}.

If the role is filled or the budget moved, no problem at all - just let me know
and I'll stop chasing.

Saif
```

Send **once**. A second follow-up on a cold thread costs more reputation than the
lead is worth.

## Sending checklist

Before any mail leaves:

1. Recipient is a real person or a real `jobs@` / `careers@` address on the
   company's own domain - not scraped from an unrelated page.
2. `{SPECIFIC_REQUIREMENT_FROM_THE_AD}` is genuinely in the ad, quoted accurately.
3. The project named is one you can talk about for ten minutes under questioning.
4. The CV attached is the tailored one for this role, not the master.
5. Daily send count is still under 12.
