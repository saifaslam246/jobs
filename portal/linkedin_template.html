LinkedIn Paste Sheet

Every field LinkedIn asks for, written from the same profile your CV comes from — so the two say the same thing. Click Copy, paste, next.

How to use this
  1. Open your profile on linkedin.com in another tab
  2. Work down this page: click Copy, click the matching LinkedIn field, paste
  3. Six fields. Around five minutes.
Why this isn't automatic. LinkedIn has no API for editing a profile — the only programmatic route is driving your logged-in session, which breaches their terms and gets accounts restricted. Your profile is worth more than the saved minutes, so this page does everything up to the paste and stops there.
